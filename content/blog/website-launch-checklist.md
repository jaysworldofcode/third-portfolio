A website launch should be the least exciting day of the project. If everything has been tested, flipping the switch is a formality. This is the checklist we work through before every go-live.

## Content

- Every page proofread, including buttons, form messages and error pages
- No placeholder text or images left anywhere
- Page titles and descriptions written for search results and social sharing

## Performance

- Images resized, compressed and served in modern formats
- Fonts limited to the weights we actually use
- A quick test on a mid-range phone over a slow connection

## Accessibility

- Every image has meaningful alt text (or an empty one if it's decorative)
- The whole site works with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline
- Colour contrast checked in every theme
- Animations respect the reduced-motion setting

## Technical

- Redirects from every old URL to its new home
- A sitemap and robots file in place
- Forms tested end to end, including the email that arrives in your inbox
- Analytics recording visits, and a cookie notice where the law requires one

## After launch

We keep an eye on the site for the first two weeks: error logs, search console, form submissions and real visitor speed. Small fixes in week one prevent big problems in month three.

> The best launch is the one nobody notices, apart from your new customers.
