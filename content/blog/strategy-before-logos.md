Most rebrands start with someone saying "we need a new logo". It's a fair instinct: the logo is the most visible part of a brand. But a logo is the *answer* to a set of questions, and if nobody has asked them yet, the design work turns into a guessing game.

That's why every brand project at Lorem Agency starts with a short, focused strategy phase.

## The three questions

Before we sketch anything, we want clear answers to three questions:

1. **Who is this for?** Not "everyone between 25 and 45", but the specific people whose problem you solve best.
2. **Why should they choose you?** The honest reason, in one sentence, that a competitor couldn't copy word for word.
3. **How should it feel?** Three or four words that describe the personality we're designing for.

If the answers fit on a single page, we're ready to design.

## How we get the answers

- **Stakeholder interviews.** Thirty minutes each with founders, sales and support. The best insights usually come from the people who talk to customers every day.
- **Customer conversations.** A handful of calls with real customers, asking why they bought and what nearly stopped them.
- **A competitor wall.** Screenshots of every competitor's website, ads and packaging on one board. Patterns (and gaps) jump out quickly.

## What you get

At the end of the phase you get a short strategy document: audience, positioning, personality and key messages. It becomes the brief for the identity, and a useful filter for every decision after launch.

> A good strategy doesn't make the design safer. It makes bold choices easier to defend.

The phase usually takes two weeks. It's the cheapest part of a project and the one that saves the most time later.
